# the sequential A*
astar               -         both OPEN lists on the 8-puzzle and the 15-puzzle
grid                -         random grids with 4 and 8 moves, against Dijkstra
graph               -         the map of Romania of Russell and Norvig
limits              -         the node limit and the unsolvable instances
reopen              -         an inconsistent heuristic that forces a reopening

# the best-first search with an incumbent (branch-and-bound)
bb                  -         the 0-1 knapsack against the dynamic program
bb-shared-thread    -         the shared queue, 1 to 8 workers
bb-dist-thread      -         the distributed queues, 1 to 8 workers
bb-shared-openmp    openmp    the shared queue on OpenMP
bb-dist-openmp      openmp    the distributed queues on OpenMP
bb-shared-fastflow  fastflow  the shared queue on FastFlow
bb-dist-fastflow    fastflow  the distributed queues on FastFlow

# the parallel searches on the C++ threads
hda-thread          -         HDA* with every distribution, 1 to 8 workers
spa-thread          -         SPA*, 1 to 8 workers
pbnf-thread         -         SafePBNF, 1 to 8 workers
pase-thread         -         PA*SE, 1 to 8 workers, each state expanded once

# the parallel searches on OpenMP
hda-openmp          openmp    HDA* with every distribution, 1 to 8 workers
spa-openmp          openmp    SPA*, 1 to 8 workers
pbnf-openmp         openmp    SafePBNF, 1 to 8 workers

# the parallel searches on FastFlow
hda-fastflow        fastflow  HDA* with every distribution, 1 to 8 workers
farm-fastflow       fastflow  the master-worker farm, 1 to 8 workers

# the distributed search
mpi                 mpi       HDA* over MPI, every deterministic distribution
